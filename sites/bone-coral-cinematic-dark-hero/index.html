How we make it

Every duck goes through 5 checks.

Duck body: single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber — food-grade silicone compound (for Micro/Classic) or natural rubber with EPDM additives (for Designer/Mega/Architect). No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

  1. 01

    Visual inspection

    No porosity, streaks, or short shots.

  2. 02

    Geometry

    Beak/eyes/feet present, proportions ±2%.

  3. 03

    Drop test

    Micro/Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.

  4. 04

    Smell test

    No "rubbery" smell (sign of poor vulcanisation).

  5. 05

    Gravimetry

    Mass within ±3% of the reference.

5 checks
Eyes — paint, not inserts. No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams. Black pupil + white sclera.

Indoor only.

−20 °C … +60 °C.

Above +70 °C — deformation. Below −25 °C — embrittlement (especially Mega/Architect).

Protect from direct UV (colour degrades after 2+ years) and from oils/solvents.

Wash with warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohol, or bleach.

Material certifications: EN 71-3 (chemical migration), REACH (no phthalates or bisphenol A).

B2B / Custom

Custom = picking from a branded palette.

12 branded Pantone-equivalents (TPM — Quacktory Pigment Menu).

Fully arbitrary colour (a third-party Pantone) is a separate conversation as a new formula development. Separate line in the quote, +2 weeks to the lead time, +15% to the price.

Custom geometry (a new duck shape, a brand mascot figure) is a separate project with a master model, discussed individually.

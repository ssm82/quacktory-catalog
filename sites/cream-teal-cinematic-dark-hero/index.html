How we make it

Serious ducks for un-serious people.

Turn the most mundane object in the bathroom into a piece of design, a collector's item, and an architectural gesture.

01

Material and eyes

Duck body: single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber — food-grade silicone compound (for Micro/Classic) or natural rubber with EPDM additives (for Designer/Mega/Architect). No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

Eyes — paint, not inserts.

Eye shape (oval/round/position) is invariant. The same base mould relief across every SKU.

Eye colour is invariant. Black pupil + white sclera.

Pigment is applied post-vulcanisation by hand, with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water.

02

Technology per line

Line Moulding method Tooling Cycle
Micro injection moulding steel mould, 8 cavities ~45 s/cycle
Classic compression moulding aluminium mould, 1–2 cavities ~3 min/cycle
Designer hand-casting in silicone rubber mould (for limited runs ≤300 units) or compression (for runs >300) silicone mould or aluminium 1 cycle per day per mould
Mega rotational moulding steel split mould, internal fibreglass reinforcing frame (for storage strength) ~2 h/cycle + 8 h cooling
Architect sculptural casting against a master CNC-milled polystyrene master, fibreglass mould, internal steel frame with stiffening ribs 2–4 weeks per duck

03

Quality control

Every duck goes through 5 checks (Architect gets an extended protocol):

  1. 01 Visual inspection — no porosity, streaks, or short shots.
  2. 02 Geometry — beak/eyes/feet present, proportions ±2%.
  3. 03 Drop test — Micro/Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.
  4. 04 Smell test — no “rubbery” smell (a sign of poor vulcanisation).
  5. 05 Gravimetry — mass within ±3% of the reference.

Architect additionally receives: ultrasonic inspection of the frame's welded seams, on-site assembly photo protocol.

Care

Wash with warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohol, or bleach.

B2B / Custom

Custom

Custom = picking from a branded palette.

12 branded Pantone-equivalents (TPM — Quacktory Pigment Menu), into which the mass is tinted BEFORE moulding.

Fully arbitrary colour (a third-party Pantone) is a separate conversation as a new formula development. Separate line in the quote, +2 weeks to the lead time, +15% to the price.

Custom geometry (a new duck shape, a brand mascot figure) is a separate project with a master model, discussed individually.

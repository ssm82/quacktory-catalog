Duck body

How we make it

Duck body: single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber — food-grade silicone compound (for Micro/Classic) or natural rubber with EPDM additives (for Designer/Mega/Architect).

No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams. No plastic or vinyl stickers on the body.
Eyes — paint, not inserts. The same base mould relief across every SKU.
Eye colour is invariant. Black pupil + white sclera.

Every duck goes through 5 checks

  1. Visual inspection

    no porosity, streaks, or short shots.

  2. Geometry

    beak/eyes/feet present, proportions ±2%.

  3. Drop test

    Micro/Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.

  4. Smell test

    no “rubbery” smell (sign of poor vulcanisation).

  5. Gravimetry

    mass within ±3% of the reference.

Architect additionally receives: ultrasonic inspection of the frame's welded seams, on-site assembly photo protocol.

Every SKU ships with a care card + a QR code pointing to this Brand DNA page.

Material certifications

Compliance

Material certifications

EN 71-3 (chemical migration), REACH (no phthalates or bisphenol A).

Temperature range

−20 °C … +60 °C. Above +70 °C — deformation. Below −25 °C — embrittlement (especially Mega/Architect).

Environment

indoor only. Protect from direct UV (colour degrades after 2+ years) and from oils/solvents.

Care

wash with warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohol, or bleach.

Age marking on the packaging is mandatory:

  • Micro 4 cm — 6+, separate sticker — Not for children under 3. Small part.
  • Classic — 3+.
  • Designer — 6+ (collector's object, not a toy).
  • Mega — Not a toy. Decorative object.
  • Architect — Installation. Mounting by certified crew only.

B2B / Custom development

Custom development

12 branded Pantone-equivalents (TPM — Quacktory Pigment Menu), into which the mass is tinted BEFORE moulding. This is not “paint on top” (annoys buyers with durability and price) — it is body-through pigment. Lead time and price are predictable.

Fully arbitrary colour

(a third-party Pantone) is a separate conversation as a new formula development. Separate line in the quote, +2 weeks to the lead time, +15% to the price.

Custom geometry

(a new duck shape, a brand mascot figure) is a separate project with a master model, discussed individually.

№ 04 — Production

How we make ducks. Full factology, no marketing.

Single-piece moulding, no plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams. The eyes are food-grade silicone pigment applied by hand after vulcanisation. Black pupil, white sclera, one mould relief across every SKU.

// material

Body and eyes

Vulcanised rubber: food-grade silicone compound for Micro/Classic, natural rubber with EPDM additives for Designer/Mega/Architect. The eyes are pigment, not inserts. UV- and water-resistant, applied by hand.

// cast

Method by lineup

  • Microinjection · 45 sec/cycle
  • Classiccompression · 3 min/cycle
  • Designerhand-poured into flask
  • Megarotational · 2 h + 8 h cooling
  • Architectsculptural casting · 2–4 wks
// QC · 5 checks

Each duck goes through

  1. Visual inspection — no pores, no streaks, no under-fill.
  2. Geometry — beak, eyes, feet in place; proportions ±2%.
  3. Drop test — 5× from 1 m onto concrete, no cracks.
  4. Smell test — no rubbery off-odour (bad vulcanisation).
  5. Gravimetric check — mass within ±3% of the master.
// compliance

Certifications and conditions

GOST 25779-83 (rubber toys) · EN 71-3 (chemical migration) · REACH (phthalate-free and BPA-free). Temperature range: −20 °C … +60 °C. Indoor use only. Keep out of direct UV and oils. Architect — extended protocol with ultrasonic inspection of the frame and a photo log of the install.

№ 06 — Care

Care.

  • Wash in warm water with mild soap.
  • Do not use abrasives, alcohols or bleach.
  • Indoor use only. Direct UV exposure — colour degrades over 2+ years.
  • Temperature range: −20 °C … +60 °C. Above +70 °C — deformation; below −25 °C — embrittlement (especially for Mega/Architect).
  • Each SKU ships with a care card + QR to the full guide.

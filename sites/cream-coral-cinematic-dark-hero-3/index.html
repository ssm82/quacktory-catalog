02 / Production

Paint, not inserts.

Every duck is a single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber. No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

Material

Food-grade silicone compound for Micro / Classic. Natural rubber with EPDM additives for Designer / Mega / Architect.

Eyes
White sclera Black pupil Pigment

Pigment is applied post-vulcanisation by hand with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water.

Environment

Indoor only. Protect from direct UV and from oils / solvents.

Quality control

Every duck goes through 5 checks.

  1. 01

    Visual inspection — no porosity, streaks, or short shots.

  2. 02

    Geometry — beak / eyes / feet present, proportions ±2%.

  3. 03

    Drop test — Micro / Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.

  4. 04

    Smell test — no “rubbery” smell.

  5. 05

    Gravimetry — mass within ±3% of the reference.

Architect additionally receives: ultrasonic inspection of the frame's welded seams, on-site assembly photo protocol.

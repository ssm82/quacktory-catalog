How we make it

Every duck is a single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber — food-grade silicone compound (for Micro/Classic) or natural rubber with EPDM additives (for Designer/Mega/Architect). No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

01

Single-piece moulding

No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

02

Eyes

Paint, not inserts. Black pupil + white sclera. Pigment is applied post-vulcanisation by hand, with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water.

03

5 checks

  • Visual inspection
  • Geometry
  • Drop test
  • Smell test
  • Gravimetry
04

Compliance

EN 71-3 (chemical migration). REACH (no phthalates or bisphenol A).

05

Temperature

−20 °C … +60 °C

Above +70 °C — deformation. Below −25 °C — embrittlement. Indoor only.

06

Care

Wash with warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohol, or bleach.

Custom development

Custom = picking from a branded palette. The mass is tinted BEFORE moulding — body-through pigment, not paint on top. Lead time and price are predictable.

02

Third-party Pantone

03

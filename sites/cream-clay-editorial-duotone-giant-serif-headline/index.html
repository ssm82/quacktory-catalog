How we make them

Single-piece mould. Five checks.

The duck body is a single mould of vulcanised rubber. No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

Every duck passes a visual inspection, geometry check, drop test, smell test, and gravimetric check.

  • ±2%proportions
  • ±3%mass
  • −20…+60 °Crange

Bespoke development

12 brand colours. Solid pigment.

A fully custom colour is quoted separately: +2 weeks lead time, +15% to the price. Custom geometry is a separate project with its own master mould.

02 / How we make it

How we make it

Single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber.

Material

Duck body

Single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber — food-grade silicone compound (for Micro/Classic) or natural rubber with EPDM additives (for Designer/Mega/Architect). No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

Temperature range −20 °C … +60 °C

Eyes

Eyes — paint, not inserts.

Eye shape (oval/round/position) is invariant. The same base mould relief across every SKU. Eye colour is invariant. Black pupil + white sclera. No plastic or vinyl stickers on the body. Technically: pigment is applied post-vulcanisation by hand, with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water.

Quality control

Every duck goes through 5 checks (Architect gets an extended protocol):

  1. 01 Visual inspection — no porosity, streaks, or short shots.
  2. 02 Geometry — beak/eyes/feet present, proportions ±2%.
  3. 03 Drop test — Micro/Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.
  4. 04 Smell test — no "rubbery" smell (sign of poor vulcanisation).
  5. 05 Gravimetry — mass within ±3% of the reference.

Architect additionally receives: ultrasonic inspection of the frame's welded seams, on-site assembly photo protocol.

Temperature range

Temperature range: −20 °C … +60 °C. Above +70 °C — deformation. Below −25 °C — embrittlement (especially Mega/Architect).

Environment

Environment: indoor only. Protect from direct UV (colour degrades after 2+ years) and from oils/solvents.

Care

Care: wash with warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohol, or bleach.

Material certifications: EN 71-3 (chemical migration), REACH (no phthalates or bisphenol A).

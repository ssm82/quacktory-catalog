— Chapter 03 —

How we
make them

Solid cast. No plastic inserts. No glued seams. Eyes — painted, not stickered.

3.1

Material & eyes

The body is a single solid cast of vulcanized rubber: food-grade silicone compound for Micro and Classic, natural rubber with EPDM additives for Designer, Mega and Architect. Eyes — pigment, applied after vulcanization. UV- and water-resistant.

  • Silicone
  • Natural rubber
  • EPDM
  • UV-stable
3.2

By line

  • Micro — injection, 8 cavities, ~45 sec/cycle
  • Classic — compression, ~3 min/cycle
  • Designer — hand-laid silicone-mold forming, 1 cycle per day
  • Mega — rotational molding, ~2 h + cool-down
  • Architect — sculptural pour, master model + steel frame
3.3

Quality control

Five checks on every duck: visual inspection, geometry ±2%, drop test from 1 m, smell test, gravimetry ±3%. Architect — extended protocol with ultrasonic frame inspection and a photo-logged install.

  • ±2% geometry
  • 5-stage QC
  • Drop test
  • Ultrasonic
3.4

Compliance

GOST 25779-83, EN 71-3, REACH. Phthalate- and BPA-free. Temperature range: −20 °C … +60 °C. Indoor only. Care — warm water and mild soap.

  • GOST
  • EN 71-3
  • REACH
  • −20…+60 °C

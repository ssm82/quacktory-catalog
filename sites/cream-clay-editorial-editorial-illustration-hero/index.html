— production

How we make our ducks.

Single-piece cast from vulcanized rubber. No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams. Eyes are paint, not stickers.

01

Casting by size

Food-grade silicone for Micro and Classic. Natural rubber with EPDM additives for Designer, Mega, and Architect. Each SKU has its own tooling and its own cycle.

02

Eyes as a signature

The base mold relief is invariant. Pigment is applied by hand after vulcanization, using food-grade silicone dye that's UV- and water-resistant.

03

5 QC checks

Visual, geometry ±2%, 5 drops from 1 m, odor test, gravimetry ±3%. For Architect — ultrasonic frame check and photo installation protocol.

04

Compliance

GOST 25779-83, EN 71-3, REACH. Free of phthalates and bisphenol A. Indoor only. Operating range −20 °C … +60 °C.

05

Packaging

Recycled cardboard for small SKUs, corrugated box with insert for Mega, wooden crate with climate control for Architect.

How we make

Single-piece cast.
No seams, no inserts.

  1. 01

    Material

    Food-grade silicone for Micro/Classic. Natural rubber with EPDM — for Designer/Mega/Architect. No plastic, no metal inside, no glued seams.

  2. 02

    Mould

    Injection, compression, rotational molding — or hand-pouring into a silicone mold for limited editions. One silhouette across all SKUs.

  3. 03

    Eyes

    Pigment, not inserts. Black pupil + white sclera, applied after vulcanization with food-grade silicone dye, UV- and water-resistant.

  4. 04

    QC

    5 checks: visual, geometry ±2%, drop test, smell test, gravimetry ±3%. For Architect — ultrasonic inspection.

  5. 05

    Compliance

    GOST 25779-83, EN 71-3, REACH. No phthalates or bisphenol A. Temperature range −20…+60 °C. Indoor only.

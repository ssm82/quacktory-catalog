№ 02 — HOW WE MAKE THEM

SINGLE-PIECE × RUBBER

The body is vulcanised rubber. For Micro and Classic, a food-grade silicone compound. For Designer, Mega and Architect, natural rubber with EPDM additives. The eyes are food-grade silicone pigment over a vulcanised surface. UV- and water-resistant.

  • 01 VISUAL INSPECTION — no pores, no streaks, no under-fill.
  • 02 GEOMETRY — beak, eyes, feet in place. ±2%.
  • 03 DROP TEST — 5 × 1 m onto concrete. No cracks.
  • 04 SMELL TEST — no rubbery off-odour.
  • 05 GRAVIMETRY — ±3% of the master.

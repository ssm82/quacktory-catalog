03 · Workshop

We make ducks you don't want to drop in water, but want to put on a shelf.

Solid rubber

Vulcanized silicone or natural rubber with EPDM. No plastic inserts, no glued seams.

Eyes — pigment

Black pupil and white sclera, applied with food-grade silicone paint after vulcanization. UV- and water-resistant.

5 checks

Visual, geometry, drop test from 1 m, smell test, gravimetry ±3%. Architect — ultrasonic seam inspection and photo protocol of installation.

Casting per lineup

Micro — injection, Classic — compression, Designer — hand flask for limited editions, Mega — rotational, Architect — sculptural cast from a master model.

Temperature range

−20 °C … +60 °C. Below −25 °C — embrittlement; above +70 °C — deformation. Indoor only, protected from direct UV.

Care

Warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohols, or bleach. A care card and QR come in every box.

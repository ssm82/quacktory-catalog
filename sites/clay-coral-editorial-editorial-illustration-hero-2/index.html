CHAPTER 03 · How we make

Single-piece rubber.
No seams.

The duck body is a single cast of vulcanised rubber. No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams. Eyes are pigment, not stickers.

Material

/ 01

Food-grade silicone compound for Micro and Classic. Natural rubber with EPDM additives for Designer, Mega and Architect.

Pigment is hand-applied after vulcanisation, using a food-grade silicone dye. UV- and water-resistant.

Process

/ 02
  • Micro — injection moulding, ~45 sec/cycle
  • Classic — compression moulding, ~3 min
  • Designer — hand moulding in silicone flask
  • Mega — rotational moulding, 2 h + 8 h cooling
  • Architect — sculptural cast into a master model

Quality control

/ 03

Every duck passes five checks:

  • Visual inspection — no pits or short shots
  • Geometry — proportions within ±2%
  • Drop test — 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete
  • Odour test — no "rubber" smell
  • Gravimetry — mass within ±3%

Compliance

/ 04

GOST 25779-83, EN 71-3, REACH. Free from phthalates and bisphenol A.

Operating range: −20 °C … +60 °C. Indoor only. Care: warm water with mild soap. No abrasives, alcohol or bleach.

Making

A single piece of vulcanised rubber. Eyes painted by hand.

Material

Body: single-piece vulcanised rubber — food-grade silicone compound for Micro and Classic, natural rubber with EPDM additives for Designer, Mega and Architect. No plastic inserts. No metal inside. No glued seams.

Eyes: pigment applied post-vulcanisation by hand, with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water. Black pupil, white sclera. Eye shape is the same on every SKU; eye decoration is an optional variation.

Technology per line

  1. 01

    Visual inspection

    No porosity, streaks, or short shots.

  2. 02

    Geometry

    Beak, eyes, feet present. Proportions within ±2%.

  3. 03

    Drop test

    Five drops from one metre onto concrete, no cracks.

  4. 04

    Smell test

    No rubbery smell — sign of poor vulcanisation.

  5. 05

    Gravimetry

    Mass within ±3% of the reference weight.

Care: warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, no alcohol, no bleach. Indoor only. Avoid direct UV and oils. Operating range −20 °C to +60 °C.

03

Bespoke

Twelve branded pigments, tinted into the rubber before moulding.

Custom = picking from the menu

Bespoke work for brand managers, hotels, events and retail begins with the TPM — twelve branded Quacktory Pigment Menu colours. The mass is tinted into the rubber before moulding, not painted on top. Durable. Predictable.

Fully arbitrary Pantone match is a separate formula development: add two weeks to the lead time and roughly fifteen per cent to the price. New geometry — a new duck shape or a brand mascot — is a separate project, discussed individually.

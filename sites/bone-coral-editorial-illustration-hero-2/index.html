How we make it

Duck body

Duck body

Material and eyes

Single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber — food-grade silicone compound (for Micro/Classic) or natural rubber with EPDM additives (for Designer/Mega/Architect).

No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

Eyes — paint, not inserts.

The same base mould relief across every SKU.

  • Eye shape (oval/round/position) is invariant.
  • Eye colour is invariant.
  • Black pupil + white sclera.

Pigment is applied post-vulcanisation by hand, with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water.

Quality control

Every duck goes through 5 checks (Architect gets an extended protocol):

Architect additionally receives: ultrasonic inspection of the frame's welded seams, on-site assembly photo protocol.

  1. 1 Visual inspection — no porosity, streaks, or short shots.
  2. 2 Geometry — beak/eyes/feet present, proportions ±2%.
  3. 3 Drop test — Micro/Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.
  4. 4 Smell test — no "rubbery" smell (sign of poor vulcanisation).
  5. 5 Gravimetry — mass within ±3% of the reference.

Material certifications

EN 71-3 (chemical migration), REACH (no phthalates or bisphenol A).

Age marking on the packaging is mandatory:

  • Micro 4 cm — "6+"
  • Micro 4 cm — separate sticker — "Not for children under 3. Small part."
  • Classic — "3+".
  • Designer — "6+ (collector's object, not a toy)".
  • Mega — "Not a toy. Decorative object."
  • Architect — "Installation. Mounting by certified crew only."

Care

Indoor only.

Protect from direct UV (colour degrades after 2+ years) and from oils/solvents.

Wash with warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohol, or bleach.

Temperature range

−20 °C … +60 °C.

Above +70 °C — deformation. Below −25 °C — embrittlement (especially Mega/Architect).

+60 °C +70 °C −25 °C

Wholesale

B2B / Custom

Custom = picking from a branded palette.

12 branded Pantone-equivalents (TPM — Quacktory Pigment Menu), into which the mass is tinted BEFORE moulding.

Fully arbitrary colour

Fully arbitrary colour (a third-party Pantone) is a separate conversation as a new formula development.

Separate line in the quote, +2 weeks to the lead time, +15% to the price.

Custom geometry

Custom geometry (a new duck shape, a brand mascot figure) is a separate project with a master model, discussed individually.

Lead time

  • Micro / Classic 3 weeks
  • Designer 6 weeks
  • Mega 6 weeks
  • Architect 12 weeks

04 — Production

Single-piece cast. Food-grade silicone and natural rubber. No plastic, no glue, no metal inside.

  1. i.

    Material

    Food-grade silicone (Micro / Classic) or natural rubber with EPDM (Designer / Mega / Architect). Vulcanized single-piece body, no seams.

  2. ii.

    Eyes

    Black pupil and white sclera — pigment is applied by hand after vulcanization. UV- and water-resistant.

  3. iii.

    Quality control

    5 checks on every duck: visual, geometry ±2%, drop test, odor, gravimetry ±3%.

  4. iv.

    Compliance

    GOST 25779-83, EN 71-3, REACH. Free of phthalates and bisphenol A. Operating range −20…+60 °C.

  5. v.

    Care

    Warm water, mild soap. No abrasives, alcohols, or bleach. Indoor only.

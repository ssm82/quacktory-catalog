§ Production

How we make a duck.

01

Material & eyes

Single-piece vulcanised rubber moulding. Eye decoration painted by hand with food-grade silicone dye, post-vulcanisation. No plastic, no inserts, no stickers.

Moulding per line

02

Micro: injection, 8 cavities, 45 s/cycle. Classic: compression, 3 min. Designer: hand-cast in silicone (≤300) or compression. Mega: rotational, 2 h + 8 h cooling. Architect: sculptural casting against a CNC-milled master, 2–4 weeks per duck.

03

QC & compliance

Five checks per duck. EN 71-3, REACH, no phthalates, no bisphenol A. Age marking on every pack: Micro 6+, Classic 3+, Designer 6+, Mega decorative, Architect installation only.

04

Care & temperature

Indoor only. −20 °C … +60 °C. Above +70 °C — deformation. Below −25 °C — embrittlement (especially Mega and Architect). Wash with warm water and mild soap.

05

Packaging

Recycled cardboard across every line. Designer ships with an embossed paper certificate. Architect ships in a wooden crate with climate control. Care card and a QR code in every box.

06

Lead time

Micro: today. Classic: 3 weeks. Designer: 6 weeks (design sign-off with the collab author). Mega: 6 weeks. Architect: 12 weeks including on-site survey and installation.

§ B2B · custom

Your brand colour, through the body.

Twelve branded Pantone-equivalents — the Quacktory Pigment Menu. Pigment is mixed into the mass before moulding, not painted on the surface. Durable, predictable, contract-grade. Fully arbitrary colour is a separate conversation: new formula, +2 weeks, +15%.

How we make ducks

Every duck goes through 5 checks. Architect gets an extended protocol.

01

Visual inspection

no porosity, streaks, or short shots.

02

Geometry

beak/eyes/feet present, proportions ±2%.

03

Drop test

Micro/Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.

04

Smell test

no “rubbery” smell (sign of poor vulcanisation).

05

Gravimetry

mass within ±3% of the reference.

Architect additionally receives: ultrasonic inspection of the frame's welded seams, on-site assembly photo protocol.

B2B / Custom development

Custom = picking from a branded palette.

12 branded Pantone-equivalents (TPM — Quacktory Pigment Menu), into which the mass is tinted BEFORE moulding. This is not “paint on top” (annoys buyers with durability and price) — it is body-through pigment.

Lead times

Micro / Classic 3 weeks
Designer 6 weeks (design sign-off with the collab author)
Mega 6 weeks
Architect 12 weeks (including on-site survey and installation)

Fully arbitrary colour (a third-party Pantone)

Fully arbitrary colour (a third-party Pantone) is a separate conversation. As a new formula development, it adds +2 weeks to the lead time, +15% to the price.

Custom geometry (a new duck shape, a brand mascot figure) is a separate project with a master model, discussed individually.

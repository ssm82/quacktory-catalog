04 / How it's made

One mould.
Five sizes.
No plastic inside.

  1. 01

    Material

    Single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber. Food-grade silicone compound for Micro and Classic, natural rubber with EPDM additives for Designer, Mega and Architect. No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

  2. 02

    Eyes

    Painted, not inserted. Black pupil and white sclera, the same base mould relief across every SKU. Pigment is applied by hand post-vulcanisation with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water.

  3. 03

    Moulding

    Injection for Micro. Compression for Classic. Hand-casting in silicone for Designer runs under 300. Rotational moulding for Mega, with an internal fibreglass frame. Sculptural casting against a CNC-milled master for Architect.

  4. 04

    Quality control

    Five checks per duck: visual inspection (no porosity, streaks, short shots), geometry (beak, eyes, feet present, proportions within ±2%), drop test for Micro and Classic, smell test, gravimetry within ±3%. Architect gets ultrasonic inspection of the welded seams and an on-site assembly photo protocol.

  5. 05

    Compliance

    EN 71-3 for chemical migration, REACH for no phthalates or bisphenol A. Operating range −20 °C to +60 °C. Indoor only, protect from direct UV and from oils or solvents. Wash with warm water and mild soap, no abrasives, alcohol or bleach.

  6. 06

    Packaging

    Recycled cardboard and paper-pulp inserts. No polyethylene. A care card and a QR code ship with every duck. Mega ships on a pallet. Architect ships in a wooden crate with climate control, installed by a specialised crew.

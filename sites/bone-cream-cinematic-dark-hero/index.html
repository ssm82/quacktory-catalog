Production

How we make it

Every duck goes through 5 checks (Architect gets an extended protocol):

Duck body: single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber — food-grade silicone compound (for Micro/Classic) or natural rubber with EPDM additives (for Designer/Mega/Architect). No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

Technology per line

01 Micro injection moulding / steel mould, 8 cavities / ~45 s/cycle
02 Classic compression moulding / aluminium mould, 1–2 cavities / ~3 min/cycle
03 Designer hand-casting in silicone rubber mould (for limited runs ≤300 units) or compression (for runs >300) / silicone mould or aluminium / 1 cycle per day per mould
04 Mega rotational moulding / steel split mould, internal fibreglass reinforcing frame (for storage strength) / ~2 h/cycle + 8 h cooling
05 Architect sculptural casting against a master / CNC-milled polystyrene master, fibreglass mould, internal steel frame with stiffening ribs / 2–4 weeks per duck
  1. 1 Visual inspection no porosity, streaks, or short shots.
  2. 2 Geometry beak/eyes/feet present, proportions ±2%.
  3. 3 Drop test Micro/Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.
  4. 4 Smell test no "rubbery" smell (sign of poor vulcanisation).
  5. 5 Gravimetry mass within ±3% of the reference.

Eyes — paint, not inserts.

Eye shape (oval/round/position) is invariant. The same base mould relief across every SKU. Eye colour is invariant. Black pupil + white sclera.

One vector silhouette for the duck

body contour, beak, feet, eye position. Body proportions do not scale linearly with size: the Architect duck must read as the same duck as the Micro — not like a slightly larger one.

B2B / Custom

12 branded Pantone-equivalents

Custom = picking from a branded palette.

TPM — Quacktory Pigment Menu, into which the mass is tinted BEFORE moulding.

This is not "paint on top" (annoys buyers with durability and price) — it's body-through pigment. Lead time and price are predictable.

Fully arbitrary colour (a third-party Pantone) is a separate conversation as a new formula development. Separate line in the quote, +2 weeks to the lead time, +15% to the price.

Custom geometry (a new duck shape, a brand mascot figure) is a separate project with a master model, discussed individually.

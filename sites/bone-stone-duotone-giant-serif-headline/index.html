Production

How we make it

Single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber — food-grade silicone compound (for Micro/Classic) or natural rubber with EPDM additives (for Designer/Mega/Architect). No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

Eyes — paint, not inserts. Pigment is applied post-vulcanisation by hand, with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water.

  1. 01

    Visual inspection

    no porosity, streaks, or short shots.

  2. 02

    Geometry

    beak/eyes/feet present, proportions ±2%.

  3. 03

    Drop test

    Micro/Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.

  4. 04

    Smell test

    no "rubbery" smell (sign of poor vulcanisation).

  5. 05

    Gravimetry

    mass within ±3% of the reference.

5 checks

Architect additionally receives: ultrasonic inspection of the frame's welded seams, on-site assembly photo protocol.

Temperature range −20 °C … +60 °C
Environment indoor only

Protect from direct UV (colour degrades after 2+ years) and from oils/solvents.

Wash with warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohol, or bleach.

B2B / Custom development

Custom = picking from a branded palette.

12 branded Pantone-equivalents (TPM — Quacktory Pigment Menu), into which the mass is tinted BEFORE moulding.

This is not "paint on top" (annoys buyers with durability and price) — it is body-through pigment. Lead time and price are predictable.

Lead time

  • Micro / Classic 3 weeks
  • Designer 6 weeks
  • Mega 6 weeks
  • Architect 12 weeks

Fully arbitrary colour (a third-party Pantone) is a separate conversation as a new formula development. Separate line in the quote, +2 weeks to the lead time, +15% to the price.

Custom geometry (a new duck shape, a brand mascot figure) is a separate project with a master model, discussed individually.

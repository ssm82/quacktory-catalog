How we make a duck

One-piece vulcanised rubber. No plastic inserts. No glued seams. Eyes painted, not stuck on.

3.1

Material

Single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber. Food-grade silicone compound for Micro and Classic. Natural rubber with EPDM additives for Designer, Mega, and Architect.

[ INVARIANT ]
3.2

Eyes

Black pupil, white sclera. Pigment applied post-vulcanisation by hand with food-grade silicone dye. UV and water resistant.

[ PAINTED, NOT STUCK ]

Quality control

  • 01Visual — no porosity, streaks, short shots
  • 02Geometry — proportions ± 2%
  • 03Drop test — 5 drops from 1 m
  • 04Smell test — no off-gassing

How we make our ducks

Solid rubber

Silicone or natural EPDM rubber. No inserts or glued seams.

Eyes — pigment

Black pupil + white sclera. Food-grade silicone dye applied post-vulcanization.

Five inspections

Visual, geometry, 1 m drop test, odor test, gravimetry ±3%.

Casting by size

Micro — injection. Classic — compression. Designer — hand-poured flask. Mega — rotational. Architect — sculptural.

Temperature

−20 °C … +60 °C. Indoor use only. Protected from direct UV.

Care

Warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohols, or bleach.

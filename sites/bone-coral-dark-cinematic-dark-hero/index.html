PRODUCTION / HOW IT IS MADE

Material · Mould · Eyes · QC
3.1

Material

Single-piece cast from vulcanised rubber. Food-grade silicone compound for Micro/Classic, natural rubber with EPDM additives for Designer/Mega/Architect. No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

3.2

Tech by line

Micro — injection, ~45 sec/cycle. Classic — compression, ~3 min. Designer — hand-casting in a silicone mould. Mega — rotational moulding with an internal fibreglass frame. Architect — sculptural casting with a master model and a steel frame.

3.3

QC / 5 checks

Visual inspection. Geometry ±2%. Drop from 1 m onto concrete. Smell test. Gravimetry ±3%. For Architect — ultrasonic testing of the frame's welded seams and a photo protocol of the installation.

3.4

Compliance

GOST 25779-83, EN 71-3, REACH. No phthalates. No bisphenol A. Temperature range −20 … +60 °C. Indoor use only. Care — warm water, mild soap, no abrasives or alcohols.

Production

How we make ducks

single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber — food-grade silicone compound (for Micro/Classic) or natural rubber with EPDM additives (for Designer/Mega/Architect).

Duck body

Single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber. No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

Eyes — paint, not inserts.

Black pupil + white sclera. Pigment is applied post-vulcanisation by hand, with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water.

Quality control

01

Visual inspection — no porosity, streaks, or short shots.

02

Geometry — beak/eyes/feet present, proportions ±2%.

03

Drop test — Micro/Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.

04

Smell test — no 'rubbery' smell (sign of poor vulcanisation).

05

Gravimetry — mass within ±3% of the reference.

Architect additionally receives: ultrasonic inspection of the frame's welded seams, on-site assembly photo protocol.

Material certifications

EN 71-3 (chemical migration), REACH (no phthalates or bisphenol A).

Temperature range

−20 °C … +60 °C. Above +70 °C — deformation. Below −25 °C — embrittlement (especially Mega/Architect).

Environment

indoor only. Protect from direct UV (colour degrades after 2+ years) and from oils/solvents.

Care

wash with warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohol, or bleach.

B2B / Custom development

Can you paint it in our brand colour?

Custom = picking from a branded palette.

12 branded Pantone-equivalents

(TPM — Quacktory Pigment Menu), into which the mass is tinted BEFORE moulding. This is not 'paint on top' (annoys buyers with durability and price) — it's body-through pigment. Lead time and price are predictable.

Fully arbitrary colour

(a third-party Pantone) is a separate conversation as a new formula development. Separate line in the quote, +2 weeks to the lead time, +15% to the price.

Custom geometry

(a new duck shape, a brand mascot figure) is a separate project with a master model, discussed individually.

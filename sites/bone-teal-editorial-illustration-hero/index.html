How we make it

Duck body

single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber — food-grade silicone compound (for Micro/Classic) or natural rubber with EPDM additives (for Designer/Mega/Architect). No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

Eyes — paint, not inserts.

Black pupil + white sclera.

Eye shape (oval/round/position) is invariant. The same base mould relief across every SKU. Eye colour is invariant.

Technically, pigment is applied post-vulcanisation by hand, with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water.

Quality control

Every duck goes through 5 checks

  1. Visual inspection — no porosity, streaks, or short shots.
  2. Geometry — beak/eyes/feet present, proportions ±2%.
  3. Drop test — Micro/Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.
  4. Smell test — no "rubbery" smell (sign of poor vulcanisation).
  5. Gravimetry — mass within ±3% of the reference.

Material certifications

EN 71-3 / REACH

EN 71-3 (chemical migration), REACH (no phthalates or bisphenol A).

Temperature range

−20 °C … +60 °C.

Above +70 °C — deformation. Below −25 °C — embrittlement (especially Mega/Architect).

Environment: indoor only. Protect from direct UV (colour degrades after 2+ years) and from oils/solvents.

Care

wash with warm water and mild soap.

No abrasives, alcohol, or bleach.

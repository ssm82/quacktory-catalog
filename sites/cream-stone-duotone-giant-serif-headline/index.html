How we make it

Duck body

Single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber

food-grade silicone compound. natural rubber with EPDM additives.

  • Micro/Classic — food-grade silicone compound.
  • Designer/Mega/Architect — natural rubber with EPDM additives.
  • No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

Eyes

Eyes — paint, not inserts.

Black pupil + white sclera.

  • Eye shape (oval/round/position) is invariant. The same base mould relief across every SKU.
  • Eye colour is invariant.
  • Pigment is applied post-vulcanisation by hand, with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water.
  • No plastic or vinyl stickers on the body.

Quality control

Every duck goes through 5 checks.

  1. Visual inspection — no porosity, streaks, or short shots.
  2. Geometry — beak/eyes/feet present, proportions ±2%.
  3. Drop test — Micro/Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.
  4. Smell test — no “rubbery” smell (sign of poor vulcanisation).
  5. Gravimetry — mass within ±3% of the reference.

Architect additionally receives: ultrasonic inspection of the frame's welded seams, on-site assembly photo protocol.

Compliance and limits

  • EN 71-3 (chemical migration)
  • REACH (no phthalates or bisphenol A)

Age marking on the packaging is mandatory:

  • Micro 4 cm — “6+”, separate sticker — “Not for children under 3. Small part.”
  • Classic — “3+”.
  • Designer — “6+ (collector's object, not a toy)”.
  • Mega — “Not a toy. Decorative object.”
  • Architect — “Installation. Mounting by certified crew only.”

Temperature range

−20 °C … +60 °C. Above +70 °C — deformation. Below −25 °C — embrittlement (especially Mega/Architect).

Environment

Indoor only. Protect from direct UV (colour degrades after 2+ years) and from oils/solvents.

Care

Wash with warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohol, or bleach.

02 / Production

Solid rubber. No inserts.

The body is cast in a single cycle. Eyes are pigment, not plastic. Five checks on every duck. Extended protocol — for Architect.

3.1
Material

Vulcanized rubber. Food-grade silicone for Micro/Classic, natural rubber with EPDM additives for Designer/Mega/Architect. No plastic inside, no metal, no glue.

  • food-grade silicone
  • EPDM
  • phthalate-free
  • bisphenol A-free
3.2
Process by line
Microinjection · 45 sec/cycle
Classiccompression · ~3 min
Designerhand flask · 1 cycle/day
Megarotational · ~2 h + 8 h cooling
ArchitectCNC master model · 2–4 weeks
3.3
QC / 5 checks
  • 01Visual inspection — no pores, smears, or short-shots
  • 02Geometry — beak, eyes, feet in place, proportions ±2%
  • 03Drop test — 5× from 1 m onto concrete, no cracks
  • 04Odor test — no "rubber" smell (poor vulcanization)
  • 05Gravimetry — mass within ±3% of the reference
3.4
Compliance

Operating range −20…+60 °C. Above +70 °C — deformation, below −25 °C — embrittlement. Indoor only. Direct UV degrades color within 2+ years.

GOST 25779-83 EN 71-3 REACH

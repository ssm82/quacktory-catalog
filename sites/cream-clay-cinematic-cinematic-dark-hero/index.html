The Artist

Quacktory is a workshop that makes ducks you don't want to let into the water — you want to put on a shelf.

The small ones go in your pocket. The big ones go in the lobby. All of them go in the frame.

The Name

Serious ducks for un-serious people. Five sizes, one silhouette, from 4 cm to 3 m — the name is a straight-faced joke, the casting is not.

The Object

The duck body is a single-piece cast in vulcanised rubber. No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

Each duck passes a visual inspection, geometry check, drop test, smell test, and gravimetric check.

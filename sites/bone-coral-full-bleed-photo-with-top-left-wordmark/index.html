How we make it

Duck body

Duck body: single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber — food-grade silicone compound (for Micro/Classic) or natural rubber with EPDM additives (for Designer/Mega/Architect). No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

Eyes — paint, not inserts.
Black pupil + white sclera. Pigment is applied post-vulcanisation by hand, with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water.
Temperature range
−20 °C … +60 °C. Above +70 °C — deformation. Below −25 °C — embrittlement (especially Mega/Architect).
Environment
indoor only. Protect from direct UV (colour degrades after 2+ years) and from oils/solvents.
Care
wash with warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohol, or bleach.

Quality control

Every duck goes through 5 checks (Architect gets an extended protocol):

  1. 01 Visual inspection — no porosity, streaks, or short shots.
  2. 02 Geometry — beak/eyes/feet present, proportions ±2%.
  3. 03 Drop test — Micro/Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.
  4. 04 Smell test — no "rubbery" smell (sign of poor vulcanisation).
  5. 05 Gravimetry — mass within ±3% of the reference.

B2B / Custom

Custom = picking from a branded palette.

12 branded Pantone-equivalents

Segment D gets 12 branded Pantone-equivalents (TPM — Quacktory Pigment Menu), into which the mass is tinted BEFORE moulding. This is not "paint on top" (annoys buyers with durability and price) — it is body-through pigment.

Custom colour from palette

Custom = picking from a branded palette. 12 branded Pantone-equivalents (TPM — Quacktory Pigment Menu), into which the mass is tinted BEFORE moulding.

Fully arbitrary colour

Fully arbitrary colour (a third-party Pantone) is a separate conversation as a new formula development. Separate line in the quote, +2 weeks to the lead time, +15% to the price.

Custom geometry

Custom geometry (a new duck shape, a brand mascot figure) is a separate project with a master model, discussed individually.

Lead times

  • Micro / Classic 3 weeks.
  • Designer 6 weeks
  • Mega 6 weeks.
  • Architect 12 weeks (including on-site survey and installation)

How we make them

Solid rubber

Silicone or natural rubber with EPDM. No inserts, no glued seams.

Eyes — pigment

Black pupil + white sclera. Food-grade silicone paint, applied after vulcanization.

Five checks

Visual, geometry, drop test from 1 m, smell test, gravimetry ±3%.

Casting by line

Micro — injection. Classic — compression. Designer — hand-flask. Mega — rotational. Architect — sculptural.

Temperature

−20 °C … +60 °C. Indoor only. Protected from direct UV.

Care

Warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohols or bleaches.

Custom

12 signature TPM colors

The pigment runs through the mass before casting — not a surface paint.

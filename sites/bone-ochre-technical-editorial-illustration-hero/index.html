§ 03 / How we make it

Single-piece moulding. No inserts. No seams.

Every duck is one piece of vulcanised rubber — food-grade silicone for the small, natural rubber with EPDM additives for the large. The eyes are painted post-vulcanisation with food-grade dye, not glued on.

01 / Material

Vulcanised rubber

Food-grade silicone for Micro and Classic. Natural rubber with EPDM additives for Designer, Mega, and Architect. No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

02 / Tooling

Right mould per run

Steel moulds for injection (8 cavities, 45 s per cycle). Aluminium for compression (3 min per cycle). Silicone rubber moulds for hand-casting limited Designer runs. CNC-milled polystyrene masters for Architect.

03 / Eyes

Painted, not inserted

Black pupil, white sclera. Applied by hand with food-grade silicone dye, post-vulcanisation. Eye shape and position are invariant across the catalog — the silhouette is the signature.

04 / QC

Five checks per duck

Visual inspection. Geometry within ±2% of reference. Drop test — 5 drops from 1 metre onto concrete. Smell test. Gravimetry within ±3% of reference mass. Architect adds ultrasonic frame inspection.

05 / Compliance

EN 71-3 · REACH

No phthalates, no bisphenol A. Indoor use. Operating range −20 °C to +60 °C. Wash with warm water and mild soap. Protect from direct UV and from oils or solvents.

06 / Logistics

Crate to lobby

Small-parcel courier for Micro, Classic, and Designer. Pallet delivery for Mega. Wooden crate with climate control for Architect, with on-site installation by certified crew.

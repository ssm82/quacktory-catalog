04 · Production

How we make it

Solid rubber

Silicone or natural rubber with EPDM. No inserts, no glued seams.

Eyes — pigment

Black pupil + white sclera. Food-grade silicone pigment applied after vulcanization.

Five checks

Visual, geometry, 1 m drop test, smell test, gravimetry ±3%.

Casting by line

Micro — injection. Classic — compression. Designer — hand-flask. Mega — rotational. Architect — sculptural.

Temperature

−20 °C … +60 °C. Indoor only. Protection from direct UV.

Care

Warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohols, or bleach.

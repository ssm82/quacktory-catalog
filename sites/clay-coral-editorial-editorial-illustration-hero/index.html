/ 02 — Production

How we make ducks.

Facts, not marketing. Solid cast, hand-painted eyes, five QC stages on every duck. Architect — an extended protocol with ultrasonic frame inspection.

01

Material

Solid cast from vulcanized rubber. Food-grade silicone compound for Micro/Classic, natural rubber with EPDM additives for Designer/Mega/Architect.

  • no plastic inserts
  • no metal inside
  • no glued seams
02

Eyes

Painted, not inset. Pigment is applied by hand after vulcanization, with a food-grade silicone paint that's resistant to UV and water.

  • black pupil + white sclera
  • UV- and water-resistant
  • one mold-relief across all SKUs
03

5-step QC

Every duck goes through five checks. For Architect — an extended protocol with ultrasonic inspection of frame welds and a photo-logged install.

  • visual inspection
  • geometry ±2%
  • drop test from 1 m
  • smell test
  • gravimetry ±3%
04

Compliance

Certified for RU and EU markets. Phthalate- and BPA-free. Age marking on packaging is mandatory.

  • GOST 25779-83
  • EN 71-3
  • REACH
05

Care

Warm water, mild soap. Indoor only. Temperature range −20 °C … +60 °C. Protect from direct UV and from oils/solvents.

  • no abrasives, no alcohols
  • −25 °C — embrittlement
  • +70 °C — deformation

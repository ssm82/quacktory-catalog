01 — The brief

A rubber duck is the smallest monument we will ever own. Quacktory treats it as one.

Five sizes, one silhouette. Warm amber porcelain, hand-finished in a small workshop outside Porto, then signed and numbered. The smallest hangs from a keychain; the largest stands taller than a child. Every size carries the same eye, the same posture, the same dimpled base — only the scale changes.

The collection was photographed in a single afternoon against one warm backdrop. No retouching, no composites, no second tries. The grain is the grain.

03 — The process

Cast in warm amber porcelain. Signed and numbered.

  1. 01. Slip-cast in matte porcelain, fired at 1240°C.
  2. 02. Hand-painted in warm amber, sealed with a satin ivory coat.
  3. 03. Signed under the base with edition number and date.

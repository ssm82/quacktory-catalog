Material and eyes

How we make it

Turn the most mundane object in the bathroom into a piece of design, a collector's item, and an architectural gesture.

01

Duck body

Single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber.

food-grade silicone compound (for Micro/Classic) or natural rubber with EPDM additives (for Designer/Mega/Architect). No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

02

Eyes — paint, not inserts.

The same base mould relief across every SKU. Black pupil + white sclera. Pigment is applied post-vulcanisation by hand, with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water.

Quality control

Every duck goes through 5 checks

Architect gets an extended protocol. Architect additionally receives: ultrasonic inspection of the frame's welded seams, on-site assembly photo protocol.

  1. 01 Visual inspection no porosity, streaks, or short shots.
  2. 02 Geometry beak/eyes/feet present, proportions ±2%.
  3. 03 Drop test Micro/Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.
  4. 04 Smell test no "rubbery" smell (sign of poor vulcanisation).
  5. 05 Gravimetry mass within ±3% of the reference.
Material certifications EN 71-3 chemical migration.
Material certifications REACH no phthalates or bisphenol A.
Temperature range −20 °C … +60 °C Above +70 °C — deformation. Below −25 °C — embrittlement (especially Mega/Architect).
Environment Indoor only. Protect from direct UV (colour degrades after 2+ years) and from oils/solvents.
Care Wash with warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohol, or bleach.

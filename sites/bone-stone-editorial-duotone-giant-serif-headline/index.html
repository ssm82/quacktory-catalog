THE CREDO

We make ducks you don't want to drop in the water — you want to put them on a shelf.

The small ones go in a pocket. The large ones go in a lobby. All of them go on camera.

Serious ducks for un-serious people.

THE SCALE LADDER

From 4 cm to 3 metres. One silhouette, all sizes.

The body proportions do not scale linearly with size. The Architect duck must read as the same duck as the Micro — not as a slightly larger one.

HOW WE MAKE DUCKS

Single-piece rubber. No inserts. No glued seams.

Vulcanised food-grade silicone for the small. Natural rubber with EPDM additives for the large. Eyes painted, not inserted.

01 — MATERIAL

One compound, two recipes

Micro and Classic use a food-grade silicone compound. Designer, Mega, and Architect use natural rubber with EPDM additives. Single-piece moulding. No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

02 — EYES

Painted, not inserted

The eye shape and palette are invariant across every SKU — black pupil, white sclera, the same base mould relief. Pigment is applied post-vulcanisation by hand, with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water.

03 — MOULDING

Five methods, one silhouette

Injection for Micro. Compression for Classic. Hand-casting in silicone moulds for Designer. Rotational with internal fibreglass frame for Mega. Sculptural casting against a CNC-milled polystyrene master for Architect.

04 — QUALITY CONTROL

Five checks on every duck

Visual, geometry, drop, smell, gravimetry. Architect gets an extended protocol: ultrasonic inspection of the welded frame seams, and an on-site assembly photo record.

05 — COMPLIANCE

EN 71-3, REACH

No phthalates. No bisphenol A. Operating range −20 °C to +60 °C. Indoor only — direct UV degrades colour after 2+ years. Age marking on every package: 3+ for Classic, 6+ for Micro and Designer, "not a toy" for Mega, "installation" for Architect.

06 — CARE

Warm water. Mild soap.

No abrasives. No alcohol. No bleach. Wash by hand, dry with a soft cloth. Avoid oils and solvents. Every duck ships with a care card and a QR code to the full spec sheet.

FOR BRANDS, HOTELS, EVENTS

Custom colour from a 12-pigment palette. Body-through tint, not paint.

The mass is tinted before moulding, not painted on top. Predictable lead time, predictable price.

Standard palette — TPM

12 branded Pantone-equivalents. The mass is tinted before moulding — durable, consistent, and not subject to flaking or fading like surface paint.

Third-party Pantone

A specific Pantone that is not on the TPM is a separate line in the quote. New formula development. +2 weeks to the lead time. +15% to the price.

Custom geometry

A new duck shape or a brand mascot figure is its own project. Master model, fibreglass mould, internal steel frame, on-site survey. Discussed individually.

Logistics

Micro and Classic ship as small parcels. Designer ships in an embossed box with a paper certificate. Mega requires a pallet and a courier visit. Architect ships in a wooden crate with climate control and on-site installation by a certified crew.

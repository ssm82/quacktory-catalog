How we make it

The duck body: single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber. No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

Duck body food-grade silicone compound (Micro/Classic) or natural rubber with EPDM additives (Designer/Mega/Architect).
Eyes Black pupil + white sclera. Pigment is applied post-vulcanisation by hand, with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water.
Quality control 5 checks. Visual inspection. Geometry. Drop test. Smell test. Gravimetry.
Material certifications EN 71-3 (chemical migration), REACH (no phthalates or bisphenol A).
Environment indoor only. Protect from direct UV (colour degrades after 2+ years) and from oils/solvents.
Care Wash with warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohol, or bleach.

5 checks

  • Visual inspection — no porosity, streaks, or short shots.
  • Geometry — beak/eyes/feet present, proportions ±2%.
  • Drop test — Micro/Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.
  • Smell test — no "rubbery" smell (sign of poor vulcanisation).
  • Gravimetry — mass within ±3% of the reference.

Architect additionally receives: ultrasonic inspection of the frame's welded seams, on-site assembly photo protocol.

B2B / Custom

Custom = picking from a branded palette.

Quacktory Pigment Menu

12 branded Pantone-equivalents (TPM — Quacktory Pigment Menu), into which the mass is tinted BEFORE moulding.

Fully arbitrary colour

Fully arbitrary colour (a third-party Pantone) is a separate conversation: a new formula development. Separate line in the quote, +2 weeks to the lead time, +15% to the price.

Custom geometry

Custom geometry (a new duck shape, a brand mascot figure) is a separate project with a master model, discussed individually.

PRODUCTION SECRET

HOW WE make DUCKS

MATERIAL

Solid cast in vulcanized rubber. Food-grade silicone compound for Micro and Classic. Natural rubber with EPDM additives for Designer, Mega, and Architect.

EYES

Pigment is applied after vulcanization by hand, with food-grade silicone dye. UV- and water-resistant. The eye shape is an invariant across all SKUs.

5 CHECKS

Visual inspection, geometry ±2%, 5 drops × 1 m, odor test, gravimetry ±3%. For Architect — ultrasonic inspection of welded seams.

CERT

GOST 25779-83 · EN 71-3 · REACH. Phthalate- and BPA-free. Indoor only. Temperature range −20 °C … +60 °C.

CONTRACT · CUSTOM

CUSTOM IS a palette

12 signature TPM colors from the Quacktory Pigment Menu. Pigment in the mass before casting. Durability and timelines are predictable. Custom Pantone — +2 weeks, +15%. Custom geometry — a separate project.

How we make it

Duck body: single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber — food-grade silicone compound (for Micro/Classic) or natural rubber with EPDM additives (for Designer/Mega/Architect).

Duck body

No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams.

Eyes — paint, not inserts.

Black pupil + white sclera.

pigment is applied post-vulcanisation by hand, with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water.

No plastic or vinyl stickers on the body.

Quality control

Every duck goes through 5 checks (Architect gets an extended protocol):

  • Visual inspection — no porosity, streaks, or short shots.
  • Geometry — beak/eyes/feet present, proportions ±2%.
  • Drop test — Micro/Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.
  • Smell test — no "rubbery" smell (sign of poor vulcanisation).
  • Gravimetry — mass within ±3% of the reference.

Architect additionally receives: ultrasonic inspection of the frame's welded seams, on-site assembly photo protocol.

Technology per line

  • Microinjection moulding · steel mould, 8 cavities · ~45 s/cycle
  • Classiccompression moulding · aluminium mould, 1–2 cavities · ~3 min/cycle
  • Designerhand-casting in silicone rubber mould (for limited runs ≤300 units) or compression (for runs >300) · silicone mould or aluminium · 1 cycle per day per mould
  • Megarotational moulding · steel split mould, internal fibreglass reinforcing frame (for storage strength) · ~2 h/cycle + 8 h cooling
  • Architectsculptural casting against a master · CNC-milled polystyrene master, fibreglass mould, internal steel frame with stiffening ribs · 2–4 weeks per duck

Material certifications

EN 71-3 (chemical migration), REACH (no phthalates or bisphenol A).

Temperature range

−20 °C … +60 °C. Above +70 °C — deformation. Below −25 °C — embrittlement (especially Mega/Architect).

Environment

Indoor only. Protect from direct UV (colour degrades after 2+ years) and from oils/solvents.

Care: wash with warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohol, or bleach.

Age marking on the packaging is mandatory:

  • Micro 4 cm — "6+", separate sticker — "Not for children under 3. Small part."
  • Classic — "3+".
  • Designer — "6+ (collector's object, not a toy)".
  • Mega — "Not a toy. Decorative object."
  • Architect — "Installation. Mounting by certified crew only."

Brand manager / event

12 branded Pantone-equivalents (TPM — Quacktory Pigment Menu), into which the mass is tinted BEFORE moulding.

Fully arbitrary colour

(a third-party Pantone) is a separate conversation as a new formula development.

Separate line in the quote, +2 weeks to the lead time, +15% to the price.

Custom geometry

(a new duck shape, a brand mascot figure) is a separate project with a master model, discussed individually.

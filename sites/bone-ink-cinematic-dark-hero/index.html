Production

How we make it

Duck body

single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber — food-grade silicone compound (Micro / Classic) or natural rubber with EPDM additives (Designer / Mega / Architect).

Eyes — paint, not inserts.

Eye shape (oval/round/position) is invariant. The same base mould relief across every SKU. Eye colour is invariant. Black pupil + white sclera. No plastic or vinyl stickers on the body.

Duck body Eyes — paint, not inserts. beak/eyes/feet
Micro injection moulding ~45 s/cycle
Classic compression moulding ~3 min/cycle
Designer hand-casting in silicone rubber mould (for limited runs ≤300 units) or compression (for runs >300) 1 cycle per day per mould
Mega rotational moulding ~2 h/cycle + 8 h cooling
Architect sculptural casting against a master 2–4 weeks per duck

Quality control

Every duck goes through 5 checks

  1. 01 Visual inspection — no porosity, streaks, or short shots.
  2. 02 Geometry — beak/eyes/feet present, proportions ±2%.
  3. 03 Drop test — Micro/Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.
  4. 04 Smell test — no "rubbery" smell (sign of poor vulcanisation).
  5. 05 Gravimetry — mass within ±3% of the reference.

Architect

Architect additionally receives: ultrasonic inspection of the frame's welded seams, on-site assembly photo protocol.

pigment is applied post-vulcanisation by hand, with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water.

Material certifications

EN 71-3 (chemical migration), REACH (no phthalates or bisphenol A).

Temperature range

−20 °C … +60 °C. Above +70 °C — deformation. Below −25 °C — embrittlement (especially Mega/Architect).

Environment

indoor only. Protect from direct UV (colour degrades after 2+ years) and from oils/solvents.

Care

wash with warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohol, or bleach.

custom colour from palette, wholesale, 1.5–3 m logistics

B2B / Custom development

Custom = picking from a branded palette.

This is not "paint on top" (annoys buyers with durability and price) — it is body-through pigment. Lead time and price are predictable.

EDITION 01 IS A STUDY IN RESTRAINT. FIVE OBJECTS, ONE SILHOUETTE, NOISE REMOVED UNTIL WHAT REMAINS IS SHAPE, SCALE AND MATERIAL — A QUIET PARTNER FOR EVERY SHELF, EVERY DESK, EVERY LOBBY.

WE TOOK THE FAMILIAR RUBBER DUCK AND WITHDREW THE NOISE. NO PLASTIC GLOSS, NO CARTOON SMILE, NO LOUD COLOUR. INSTEAD — CHARCOAL, IVORY, GRAPHITE, TAUPE, CONCRETE. THE COLOURWAY IS EDITORIAL. THE SHAPE STAYS THE SAME. THE POINT IS THAT IT COULD LIVE ON A SHELF NEXT TO A BOOK.

THE LADDER SCALES FROM A KEYCHAIN-SIZED 4 CM TO A 3-METRE STATEMENT PIECE. ALL FIVE SHARE THE SAME SILHOUETTE — HEAD, BEAK, BODY, BASE — SO THE COLLECTION READS AS ONE OBJECT, FIVE SCALES.

MICRO FITS A POCKET. CLASSIC IS THE WORKHORSE, A 15 CM DESK COMPANION. DESIGNER IS A 20 CM EDITION WITH A BRASS ACCENT. MEGA IS A 50 CM PIECE FOR SHELVES AND STOREFRONTS. ARCHITECT IS THE 3 M VERSION — COMMISSIONED FOR LOBBIES, HOTELS, PHOTO SHOOTS, AND THE OCCASIONAL FOYER.

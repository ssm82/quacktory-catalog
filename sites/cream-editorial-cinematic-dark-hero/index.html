HOW WE MAKE DUCKS

  1. 01

    Visual inspection

    No pores, no streaks, no under-fill. Every duck is hand-checked.

  2. 02

    Geometry

    Beak, eyes, feet — all in place. Proportions within ±2% of the master.

  3. 03

    Drop test

    Micro and Classic — five drops from one metre onto concrete. No cracks.

  4. 04

    Smell test

    No rubbery off-odour. A sign of bad vulcanisation — ruled out.

  5. 05

    Gravimetric check

    Mass within ±3% of the master. Master archive — one per batch.

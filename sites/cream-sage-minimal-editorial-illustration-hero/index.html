Chapter 03 — Production

Solid pour. No seams. No inserts.

01
Material

Food-grade silicone compound for Micro and Classic, natural rubber with EPDM additives for Designer, Mega and Architect. No plastic, no metal, no glued seams.

02
Eyes

Pigment is hand-applied after vulcanization, with a food-grade silicone paint. Black pupil, white sclera. Resistant to UV and water.

03
Process

Micro — injection. Classic — compression. Designer — hand-laid mold forming. Mega — rotational molding. Architect — sculptural pour from a CNC-milled master model.

04
Quality control

5 checks: visual inspection, geometry ±2%, drop test, smell test, gravimetry ±3%. For Architect — ultrasonic frame inspection and a photo-logged install.

05
Compliance

GOST 25779-83, EN 71-3, REACH. Phthalate- and BPA-free. Temperature range −20 to +60 °C. Indoor only.

06
Care

Warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohols or bleaches. A care card and a QR for the full guide — in every box.

№ 004 — Process

Solid rubber. No seams.

The duck's body is a single cast. The eyes are pigment, hand-applied after vulcanization. No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams. The shape and placement of the eyes are invariant; we don't add eye decor because the editorial register doesn't call for it.

01 — Material

What's inside.

Food-grade silicone compound for Micro and Classic. Natural rubber with EPDM additives for Designer, Mega, and Architect. Certifications: GOST 25779-83, EN 71-3 (chemical migration), REACH — free of phthalates and bisphenol A.

02 — Mold

Tooling.

Steel for serial runs, aluminum for small runs, silicone flask for limited editions, fiberglass and steel frame for Architect. The Architect's master model is CNC-milled polystyrene.

03 — Cast

Cycle.

Injection — 45 seconds. Compression — 3 minutes. Flask — one cycle per day. Rotational — 2 hours plus 8 hours cooling. The Architect master model — 2–4 weeks per duck.

04 — Paint

Eyes.

Black pupil, white sclera. Pigment is applied by hand after vulcanization, using food-grade silicone dye that's UV- and water-resistant. One base mold relief across all SKUs.

05 — QC

Five checks.

Visual, geometry ±2%, five drops from 1 m onto concrete, odor test (no "rubber" smell), gravimetry ±3%. For Architect additionally: ultrasonic inspection of frame welds, photo installation protocol.

06 — Pack

Packaging.

Recycled cardboard without polyethylene. Paper pulp insert. Corrugated box for Mega, wooden crate with climate control for Architect. Every box includes a care card and a QR to this page.

Care.

Warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohols, or bleach. Operating range: −20 °C … +60 °C. Above +70 °C — deformation, below −25 °C — embrittlement (especially for Mega and Architect). Indoor only. No direct UV and no oils or solvents.

№ 005 — Bespoke

Custom color — from the palette.

B2B segment gets 12 signature Pantone equivalents — the Quacktory Pigment Menu. Pigment in the mass, before casting. Not surface paint. Fully custom color (third-party Pantone) is a separate new-formula development: +2 weeks to lead time, +15% to price. Custom geometry is a separate project with its own master model.

(06) — Studio

How we
make them

Solid cast rubber. No plastic inserts, no metal inside, no glued seams. The casting method is matched to the scale: injection for Micro, compression for Classic, hand-flask for limited drops, rotational for Mega, sculptural pour for Architect.

  1. 01

    Visual inspection

    No voids, no streaking, no short pours.

  2. 02

    Geometry

    Beak, eyes, feet all present. Proportions within ±2%.

  3. 03

    Drop test

    5 drops from 1 m onto concrete, no cracks.

  4. 04

    Smell test

    No "rubbery" odor — a sign of poor vulcanization.

  5. 05

    Gravimetry

    Mass within ±3% of the reference.

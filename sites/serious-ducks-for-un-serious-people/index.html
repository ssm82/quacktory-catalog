How we make it

Every duck goes through 5 checks.

Duck body

Single-piece moulding of vulcanised rubber — food-grade silicone compound (for Micro/Classic) or natural rubber with EPDM additives (for Designer/Mega/Architect).

Eyes

Paint, not inserts. The same base mould relief across every SKU. Black pupil + white sclera. Pigment is applied post-vulcanisation by hand, with food-grade silicone dye that resists UV and water. No plastic or vinyl stickers on the body.

Quality control

Every duck goes through 5 checks.

  1. 01Visual inspection — no porosity, streaks, or short shots.
  2. 02Geometry — beak/eyes/feet present, proportions ±2%.
  3. 03Drop test — Micro/Classic: 5 drops from 1 m onto concrete with no cracks.
  4. 04Smell test — no “rubbery” smell (sign of poor vulcanisation).
  5. 05Gravimetry — mass within ±3% of the reference.

Architect

Architect additionally receives: ultrasonic inspection of the frame's welded seams, on-site assembly photo protocol.

Compliance and limits

Age marking

Micro 4 cm — 6+; separate sticker — Not for children under 3. Small part.
Classic — 3+.
Designer — 6+ (collector's object, not a toy).
Mega — Not a toy. Decorative object.
Architect — Installation. Mounting by certified crew only.

Material certifications

EN 71-3 (chemical migration). REACH (no phthalates or bisphenol A).

Temperature range

−20 °C … +60 °C. Above +70 °C — deformation. Below −25 °C — embrittlement (especially Mega/Architect).

Environment

Indoor only. Protect from direct UV (colour degrades after 2+ years) and from oils/solvents.

Care

Wash with warm water and mild soap. No abrasives, alcohol, or bleach.
